# Path C: The Meeting Script (The Call a Sponsor Books After Replying)

Read `references/05-discovery-call.md` (structure) and the discovery-question bank in `references/03-sponsorship-market.md` before drafting. Load `estack-chris-voss` if installed for the negotiation layer. Inputs: the finalized offer, and ideally the specific sponsor this call is with (their reply, what's known about them).

## What this path produces

`sponsor-meeting-script.md`: a run-of-show the user can have open on a second screen during the call. Not a teleprompter script — people don't deliver written lines well live. The format:

- **Questions verbatim.** Discovery questions and closing questions are written word for word, because precise wording determines whether the other person actually thinks.
- **Everything else as talking points.** Beats to hit in the user's own words, one plain self-contained sentence per point.
- Per-section timing, plus a running-behind note: which sections to cut (the presentation) and which are untouchable (discovery, the close).

## The shape (30-minute default)

1. **Agenda contract (2 min).** PPO, verbatim, adapted to this sponsor: purpose (understand what they want from this audience, check fit), plan (questions first, short overview at the end), outcome (a frank read on fit, and the next step booked). Ask what they want covered.
2. **Discovery (12–15 min).** 8–12 questions selected from the bank for this sponsor's likely shape (recruiting vs brand vs product), ordered situation → problem → impact. Always include: what prompted them to take the call; the "typically" question offering the two likely problem shapes; when this became a priority and what it costs; what a partnership must produce to be a success; must-have benefits and past frustrations with other properties. Include one belief-fit question (what does the company believe about this audience). Talking points for the listening protocol: summarize answers back; note repeated phrases; don't accept the first surface answer.
3. **Match (5–8 min).** Talking points only, built from the offer stack: for each of the 2–3 components most likely to matter, the component in one sentence + its parallel story (a comparable sponsor with this problem and what happened). Instruction at the top of this section: present ONLY what discovery surfaced; skip the rest of the stack. Bridge questions verbatim, for stage boundaries: "Have you seen enough to make a decision?" / "What do you like about what you've seen so far?" — and the standing rule: when the answer says they're done, stop presenting and go to the close.
4. **Close: the Five Minute Drill (5 min, untouchable).** Verbatim: "So — what do you think?"; the hedge pressure-test ("knowing what you know now, is this worth more of your time?"); the timeline anchor to their real dates; the suggested next step stated as the default ("normally from here we'd build your three options from today's notes and walk them next week — does Tuesday work?"). BAMFAM: the next meeting gets booked in this meeting.
5. **After (not in the room).** Recap email within 24 hours in their words; the three-option proposal built from this call's notes.

## Negotiation layer (woven in, not a section)

Where `estack-chris-voss` is loaded, mark these moves inline at the moments they fit: an accusation audit if the org is small/new ("you probably get pitched by every student org on campus..."), labels for discovered emotions ("it sounds like the career fair burned you"), calibrated how/what questions instead of defending price, and no-oriented closes for hesitant sponsors. Price defense rule from the offer: trade scope between options, never discount in place.

## Personalization pass

If the user knows which sponsor the call is with, tailor before delivering: cut question branches that don't fit, pre-fill the "typically" shapes with their industry, pick the parallel stories nearest their space, and note their real dates (event date vs their budget cycle) in the close section.

## Done when

The file exists in run-of-show format with timings and the running-behind note; questions and closes are verbatim while everything else is talking points; the match section presents only offer components with parallel stories attached; the close ends in a booked next step, not "send materials"; and nothing contradicts the offer file.
