# Path C: The Meeting Script (The Call a Sponsor Books After Replying)

Read `references/05-discovery-call.md` first — it carries the call structure (agenda contract, situation→problem→impact questioning, parallel stories, bridge questions, the Five Minute Drill). The sponsorship discovery-question bank is in `references/03-sponsorship-market.md`. Load `estack-chris-voss` if installed. Use the finalized offer and a specific sponsor when available; otherwise draft a discovery-first script from the supplied context and label material assumptions.

**Output:** `sponsor-meeting-script.md` — a run-of-show the user has open on a second screen. Not a teleprompter; people don't deliver written lines well live. Format rules:

- **Questions verbatim** (discovery and closing questions word for word — precise wording determines whether the other person actually thinks).
- **Everything else as talking points:** one plain, self-contained sentence per beat, in the user's own words.
- **Per-section timing** plus a running-behind note: what to cut (the presentation) and what is untouchable (discovery, the close).

## The shape (30-minute default)

Build each section from reference 05's structure, filled with the sponsor's specifics when known. Adapt the timing to the actual meeting length; 30 minutes is a starting point, not a requirement:

1. **Agenda contract (2 min).** The PPO opener, verbatim, adapted to this sponsor. End by asking what they want covered.
2. **Discovery (12–15 min).** 8–12 verbatim questions from the reference-03 bank, ordered situation → problem → impact, chosen for this sponsor's likely shape (recruiting vs brand vs product). Always include: what prompted them to take the call; the "typically" question offering the two likely problem shapes; what a partnership must produce to be a success; one belief-fit question (what does the company believe about this audience). Talking points for the listening protocol: summarize back, note repeated phrases, don't accept the first surface answer.
3. **Match (5–8 min).** Talking points only: for the 2–3 offer components discovery is likely to surface, one sentence each plus a parallel story (a comparable sponsor with this problem and what happened). Standing rules at the top of the section: present ONLY what discovery surfaced, and when a bridge question ("Have you seen enough to make a decision?") says they're done, stop presenting and close.
4. **Close: the Five Minute Drill (5 min, protect when possible).** The three closing questions verbatim, ending with a specific next step the parties agree to. Book it during the meeting when appropriate; otherwise record the owner and timing for the follow-up.
5. **After (not in the room).** Recap email within 24 hours in their words; the three-option proposal built from this call's notes.

## Negotiation layer

Where `estack-chris-voss` is loaded, mark its moves inline at the moments they fit: an accusation audit if the org is small/new, labels for discovered emotions, calibrated how/what questions instead of defending price. Price defense rule from the offer: trade scope between options, never discount in place.

## Personalization pass

If the sponsor is known: cut question branches that don't fit, pre-fill the "typically" shapes with their industry, pick parallel stories nearest their space, and put their real dates (event date, budget cycle) in the close.

## Done when

The file is in run-of-show format with timings and the running-behind note; questions and closes verbatim, everything else talking points; the match section carries only components the evidence supports; and the close prompts the parties to agree a specific next step, owner, and timing. A direct draft labels gaps that need confirmation before the call.
