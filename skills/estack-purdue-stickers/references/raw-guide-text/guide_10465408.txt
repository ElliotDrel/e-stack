##
Printing as a Club or Organization

Purdue groups, clubs, and other campus organizations are considered as 1 person meaning they have a maximum monthly print allotment of 18x36in. 

- Different people from the same group may not submit print requests for the group as "individuals" in order to print more than the maximum monthly allotment. 

- If a group is found to violate this policy, they may lose sticker printing privileges. 

- The Knowledge Lab provides sticker printing as a free resource and is not capable of mass production. 

- If your group needs more than the monthly allotment, you may need to work with a commercial printer. 

If you have questions about printing for a club or organization, please contact knowledgelab@purdue.edu <mailto:knowledgelab@purdue.edu?subject=Club%2FOrganization%20Sticker%20Printing>.
