##
Sticker Shapes

[IMAGE: alt="round/geometric sticker shape" src=https://d2jv02qf7xgjwx.cloudfront.net/customers/934/images/Circle.png]

###
Geometric

Stickers that are basic geometric shapes - circles, squares, etc.

[IMAGE: alt="irregular sticker outline shape (cat)" src=https://d2jv02qf7xgjwx.cloudfront.net/customers/934/images/Irregular.png]

###
Irregular

Stickers that have detailed edges such as fine lines like the whiskers of this cat.

[IMAGE: alt="text outline shape with irregular edges" src=https://d2jv02qf7xgjwx.cloudfront.net/customers/934/images/Irregular-text.png]

###
Text

Stickers that include type, which may include handwriting, that may have complex edges.

##
Sticker Sizes

[IMAGE: alt="sticker size examples from 3 inches to 1 inch" src=https://d2jv02qf7xgjwx.cloudfront.net/customers/934/images/StickerSizes-outlined.png]

You can print your sticker design in any size (up to 18x36 inches). The graphic above is meant to be used as a frame of reference if you're not certain what size your sticker should be.

##
Material/Finish Types (without background)

[IMAGE: alt="glossy finish sticker vinyl" src=https://d2jv02qf7xgjwx.cloudfront.net/customers/934/images/Glossy.png]

###
Glossy (shiny)

This material has a shiny finish.

[IMAGE: alt="matte finish sticker vinyl" src=https://d2jv02qf7xgjwx.cloudfront.net/customers/934/images/Matte.png]

###
Matte (flat)

This material has no shine. 

[IMAGE: alt="transparent sticker vinyl" src=https://d2jv02qf7xgjwx.cloudfront.net/customers/934/images/Transparent.png]

###
Transparent

This material is clear with some shine.
