### Need to Know Info

Before you get started, keep in mind the following:

- In order to use the sticker printer, you must schedule a print time <https://calendar.lib.purdue.edu/space/182313> and submit your design for approval in advance. Walk-in printing is not available.

- The sticker material comes in rolls that are 18 inches wide, the maximum length that can be printed in a month is 36 inches so the maximum allotment for individuals and groups/clubs in 18x36in.  

- Materials and equipment are provided for free as a courtesy of Purdue University, stickers made in the Knowledge Lab may not be used for commercial purposes or mass production. 

- You are welcome to come to the Knowledge Lab to get assistance on file prep from lab staff or attend a sticker file prep workshop. 

- Sticker printing time varies by design/type. 

- If you are printing 12 inches of material or less, 30 minutes is sufficient.

- If you are printing between 12-24 inches of material, we recommend requesting 60 minutes.

- If you are printing between 24-36 inches of material, we recommend requesting 90 minutes.

### The Print Process

- Design your sticker using whatever platform you are most comfortable with (Adobe Illustrator, Canva, etc.). 

- Follow the prep your file instructions <https://guides.lib.purdue.edu/c.php?g=1398872&p=10391398> using Adobe Illustrator to make sure your document is ready for submission.

- Schedule a print time/submit your file for review.

- If your submission is accepted, your request print time will be approved. 

- If your submission is denied, you'll receive a notice on the issues with your file and what needs to be done to correct the file.

- Visit the Knowledge Lab to print your sticker!
